About This Product

Engineered For Indian Industry

Single Girder EOT Cranes utilise one bridge girder with an underslung hoist trolley running on its bottom flange — keeping structure weight, wheel loads and building costs low while retaining full-bay coverage. The girder is a torsion-free fabricated section with deflection controlled well within code limits, riding on end carriages fitted with double-flanged forged wheels machined to your rail size. The design is manufactured in accordance with IS 3177 and IS 4137 duty classifications — the right choice for medium-duty applications demanding smooth, precise handling.

Energy Tech Crane designs every crane with an adequate factor of safety in compliance with IS 807 and IS 800 structural practice. Girders are fabricated from mill-certified IS 2062 plate by qualified welders, stress-relieved at critical joints and cambered so the bridge runs level under load; hook forgings and wire ropes are accepted only against certificates, and drives are sized to your duty cycle with VFD soft start/stop for jerk-free load control across all three motions.

ETC single girder EOT cranes are built from 1 to 20 Ton as standard, with spans up to 35 metres. As a practical rule followed across the industry, loads above 20 Ton or spans beyond about 20 metres are better served by a double girder configuration — we'll recommend honestly from your numbers.

Duty class follows how hard the crane works: M3–M5 suits maintenance and intermittent workshop use, while M7–M8 covers continuous production duty. Share your lifts per hour and average load and our engineers will classify it per IS 3177 / FEM 1.001.

Squirrel cage crane-duty motors are standard for smooth, low-maintenance performance; slip ring motors are offered for heavier starting duty. All motions use fail-safe DC brakes that hold the load instantly on power failure.

The design complies with IS 3177, IS 4137 and IS 807 with FEM 1.001 mechanism classification, and every crane is load-tested at 125% of Safe Working Load before dispatch with a stamped test certificate.
