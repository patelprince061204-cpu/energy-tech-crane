About This Product

Engineered For Indian Industry

A circular crane — also called a circular EOT or radial travelling crane — runs on a curved rail fixed to the top of a cylindrical structure, such as a storage tank, clarifier, digester, silo or circular pump house, instead of the straight runway beams used by a conventional EOT crane. A single crane can then cover the entire circumference of the vessel from a fixed centre point, which is exactly what petrochemical, water-treatment and process plants need for tank maintenance, desludging and periodic inspection.

Because the girder itself is curved to match the track radius, every ETC circular crane is engineered around your specific structure — ring-beam diameter, rail gauge, wheel base and duty cycle are calculated from your GA drawing before a single plate is cut. Girders are stress-relieved after curving to remove residual fabrication stresses, and all electricals are rated for outdoor tank-farm exposure.
