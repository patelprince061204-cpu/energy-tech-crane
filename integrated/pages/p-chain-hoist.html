About This Product

Engineered For Indian Industry

For loads under 10 Ton where speed and precision matter more than brute force, chain hoists beat everything else on cost, weight and headroom. ETC chain hoists use case-hardened grade-80 load chain running over precision-machined pocket wheels for smooth, quiet lifting.

The friction slip clutch is a mechanical overload guarantee — it physically cannot lift beyond rated capacity, protecting operator, hoist and structure. Combined with dual-speed motors, it makes these hoists ideal for jig loading, die handling and assembly work.

Chain hoists are compact, lighter and ideal up to about 5 Ton with lower headroom and simpler maintenance. Above that capacity, or for high lift heights and faster speeds, a wire rope hoist is the better engineering choice.
