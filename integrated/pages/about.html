Our Story

From One Shed To A Thousand Cranes

In 2016, Mr. Ankur Pipaliya started Energy Tech Crane with a simple conviction: Indian factories deserve cranes engineered for Indian conditions — continuous duty, honest steel, and spares you can buy in any industrial market.

  • Everything in-house — girder fabrication, machining, assembly, testing and painting under one roof at Bakrol Bujarang, Ahmedabad.
  • Standards, not shortcuts — IS:3177, IS:4137, IS:807 and FEM 1.001 govern every design calculation we release.
  • Proof before dispatch — every machine is load-tested at 125% of SWL with a stamped, documented certificate.
  • Relationships over transactions — the same phone numbers have answered service calls since day one.
