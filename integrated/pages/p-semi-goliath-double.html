About This Product

Engineered For Indian Industry

Semi Goliath cranes solve an awkward but common problem: one side of the bay has a crane rail on building columns, the other side has nothing — an open yard, a lean-to, or a newer extension. Rather than build a full second runway, the crane's far end simply rides a floor rail on its own leg.

The engineering challenge is keeping both ends synchronised; ETC uses matched geared motors with VFD control and guide rollers so the bridge tracks true without crabbing. Installation typically happens without stopping your existing operations.
