About This Product

Engineered For Indian Industry

Double Girder Gantry Cranes are the heavy lifters of open yards: twin box girders spanning up to 50 metres ride on braced A-frame legs with multi-wheel equalised bogies, carrying capacities up to 200 Ton over rail-mounted runways of any length. Precast yards, heavy fabrication bays and stockyards run on machines exactly like these.

The engineering follows IS 3177 and IS 807 with IS 875 wind-load verification; long-travel drives use VFD anti-skew control to keep wide spans square, and crab-mounted winches with true vertical lift place loads precisely. Weatherproof IP-rated electricals, certified hook forgings with magnetic-particle testing, gearboxes proven on no-load trials with backlash checks, and a stress-relieved, NDT-inspected structure proof-tested at 125% SWL make this a machine built for decades of outdoor duty.
