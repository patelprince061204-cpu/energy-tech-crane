About This Product

Engineered For Indian Industry

End carriages carry the whole crane — and its load — on four wheels, so wheel quality and alignment decide how long your rails last. L-Block housings hold each wheel-bearing assembly in a machined block bolted to the carriage: when a wheel wears, you unbolt the block, slide in a spare and run again within the hour — no girder jacking, no hot works.

ETC machines mating faces and jig-drills bolt patterns so girder-to-carriage joints assemble square every time. Wheels are forged EN-8/EN-9 with hardened treads gauged to your rail size, and every carriage ships with rail sweeps, antidrop plates and buffers fitted.
